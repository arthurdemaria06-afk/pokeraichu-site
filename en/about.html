    About

    Japan, card by card.

    Poké-Raichu selects rare Pokémon cards, mostly Japanese, and presents them in France: 243 Japanese items out of 271 in stock.

    Sourcing in Japan

    The cards are bought on the spot, in Japan, in specialist shops. The selection favours Japanese editions, Special Art Rares, the 30th Celebration series and PSA 10 slabs.

    Every card is logged individually as soon as it is bought. The reference shown on its page is our inventory number: just quote it to reserve.

    Missing a card from the catalogue? We can look for it during our buying trips. Find a card in Japan.

    Graded and verifiable

    For PSA-graded cards, the certificate number appears on the page when known, with a link to PSA’s verification database. Graded French cards are graded by PCA.

    Why Raichu

    Raichu is the founder’s favourite Pokémon, No. 026 in the Pokédex. And since Pikachu evolves into Raichu with a Thunder Stone, our Pikachu have their own collection.

    A showcase, sales on eBay

    This site is our showcase: browse the stock and reserve here. The sale and payment take place on eBay, covered by the eBay Money Back Guarantee. You can also find us at fairs, and soon on consignment and in our shop in Mandelieu-la-Napoule, with two corners: Japan, for rare cards and PSA 10s, and France, for buying cards from collectors and selling singles.

    Our commitments

    • Real stock: every listing is a card in hand, with its exact quantity.
    • Detailed photos sent on request before any reservation.
    • Payment on eBay only, never by message.
    • No official artwork: listings show our own photos or a placeholder visual.
