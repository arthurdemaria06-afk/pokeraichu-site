    How to buy

    1. Open the card’s page and tap “Reserve on WhatsApp”.
    2. A ready-to-send WhatsApp message opens, with the card’s reference.
    3. We confirm availability, send photos and hold the card for 48 hours.
    4. You receive the eBay listing link and pay on eBay: tracked shipping or collection in person.
