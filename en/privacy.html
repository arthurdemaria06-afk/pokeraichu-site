    Audience measurement

    No audience measurement is active on this site.

    E-mail forms

    Forms (contact, arrival alert, card search in Japan) are sent through Netlify Forms, a service of our host Netlify, Inc. (United States). We receive what you type (name, e-mail, message, card wanted) to reply to you or notify you of an arrival, and keep it for 12 months after our last exchange. Legal basis: your request. A hidden field and a spam filter screen out automated submissions.

    Messages, payment, hosting

    Messages sent via WhatsApp or Instagram are handled by Meta under its own rules, and used by Poké-Raichu only to reply. Payments are processed by eBay. Our host Netlify keeps technical logs (IP address, pages requested) needed to run the service. To show up-to-date statuses and prices, each page also reads a small file hosted by GitHub, Inc. (United States), which then receives your IP address; no cookie is used.

    Your rights

    You may ask for access, correction, erasure or restriction of your data, object to its processing and ask for its portability, from the Contact page. You may also complain to the French data protection authority, the CNIL (cnil.fr).
