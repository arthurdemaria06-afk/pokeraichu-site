    What is added to the listed price

    • 20% VAT from the first euro, on the price, shipping and customs duty.
    • Customs duty: since 1 July 2026, a flat €3 per item category for a parcel worth €150 or less; above that, the tariff rate (2.7% for playing cards, 0% if Japanese origin is documented).
    • EU handling fee: €2 per item category since 1 November 2026, for distance sales from outside the EU.
    • Carrier fees for advancing these amounts: €2 at La Poste when paying online; often more with express carriers.

    Indicative estimate, converted at €1 = 176.15 JPY (26/10/2026). Actual costs depend on the seller, the proxy and the carrier. Sources: service-public.gouv.fr, douane.gouv.fr, La Poste, Banque de France.
