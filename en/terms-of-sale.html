    Reservation

    You reserve by message (WhatsApp, Instagram or e-mail) from the card’s page or the My selection page. It does not commit you: we confirm availability, send photos of the actual card and the eBay listing link. The card is held for 48 hours after our confirmation; without payment within that time, it goes back on sale.

    Prices and discounts

    Prices are shown in euros on each page. Prices in euros. Shipping is added according to the rates below. Discounts on a single order: −5% from 3 cards · −10% from 6 cards. The price that applies is the one in the eBay listing at the time of payment.

    When a price reduction is announced, the struck-through price is the lowest price charged during the 30 days before the reduction (article L. 112-1-1 of the French Consumer Code).

    Lightning deals (flash sales)

    Each week, a selection of cards may be offered at a reduced price for a limited time, shown on the Lightning deals page and on each card’s page (start at midnight, end at 11:59 pm, Paris time). The struck-through price is the lowest price charged in the 30 days before the flash sale. The offer applies while stock lasts (single copy unless stated otherwise): a card reserved during the sale is paid at the lightning deal price, within the 48-hour hold. These prices cannot be combined with quantity discounts; free shipping applies above the usual threshold.

    Payment

    Payment is made on eBay only, with the methods offered by eBay, or at our stand during a fair. No payment is ever requested on this site or by message.

    Right of withdrawal

    For a distance purchase, you have 14 days from receipt to withdraw, without giving any reason: ask on eBay, by message, or with the withdrawal form. Send the card back within 14 days of your request, exactly as it was sent (sleeve, toploader, reference sticker; slab intact with the same certificate number; sealed product unopened). Return costs are yours. We refund you, including the initial standard shipping cost, within 14 days of your request; we may wait until we have received the card. A loss of value caused by handling beyond what is needed to establish the nature and condition of the card may be deducted.

    There is no right of withdrawal for purchases made at a fair or show (article L. 224-59 of the French Consumer Code); a sign at our stand says so.

    Legal guarantees

    Single cards and slabs are second-hand goods: for them, a lack of conformity appearing within twelve months of delivery is presumed to have existed at the time of sale (article L. 217-7 of the French Consumer Code). Sealed products are new. The statutory notice below is given in French, as required by French law.

Durant ce délai, le consommateur n'est tenu d'établir que l'existence du défaut de conformité et non la date d'apparition de celui-ci.

    Si le consommateur demande la réparation du bien, mais que le vendeur impose le remplacement, la garantie légale de conformité est renouvelée pour une période de deux ans à compter de la date de remplacement du bien.

    Le consommateur peut obtenir une réduction du prix d'achat en conservant le bien ou mettre fin au contrat en se faisant rembourser intégralement contre restitution du bien, si :
    1° Le professionnel refuse de réparer ou de remplacer le bien ;
    2° La réparation ou le remplacement du bien intervient après un délai de trente jours ;
    3° La réparation ou le remplacement du bien occasionne un inconvénient majeur pour le consommateur, notamment lorsque le consommateur supporte définitivement les frais de reprise ou d'enlèvement du bien non conforme, ou s'il supporte les frais d'installation du bien réparé ou de remplacement ;
    4° La non-conformité du bien persiste en dépit de la tentative de mise en conformité du vendeur restée infructueuse.

    Le consommateur a également droit à une réduction du prix du bien ou à la résolution du contrat lorsque le défaut de conformité est si grave qu'il justifie que la réduction du prix ou la résolution du contrat soit immédiate.
