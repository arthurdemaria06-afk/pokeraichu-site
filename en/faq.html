    Frequently asked questions

    How to reserve, pay on eBay and receive your cards, and what the law guarantees you.

    Reserve

    How do I reserve a card?

    On the card’s page, tap “Reserve on WhatsApp”: a ready-to-send message opens, with the reference. For several cards, add them with the + button and send your selection in one message. We confirm availability and send photos of the actual card and the eBay link to pay.

    How long is a card held?

    48 hours after our confirmation. Without payment on eBay within that time, it goes back on sale. Need more time? Tell us when you reserve.

    Pay

    Why eBay?

    This site is our showcase: it presents the stock and lets you reserve. The sale and payment take place on eBay, which handles the payment and protects your purchase with the eBay Money Back Guarantee.

    How do I pay?

    On eBay only. Once your reservation is confirmed, we send you the listing link and you pay with the payment methods offered by eBay. No payment is ever made on this site or by message.

    Receive

    How are cards shipped?

    With tracking, within 2 working days of payment on eBay. Single cards: in a sleeve, inside a toploader closed with a sticker showing their reference, in a rigid envelope. Slabs and sealed products: in a tracked, insured parcel, well padded. Every card is photographed before shipping.

    How much is shipping?

    Metropolitan France: free from €100; otherwise €4.50 for 1 to 5 cards, €7.50 for 6 cards or more, €6.50 for 1 slab, €9.50 for 2 or 3 slabs, €11.50 for 1 sealed product. Europe and rest of the world: from €8.50. A single parcel for several cards; the exact amount is shown in the eBay listing. All rates.

    Is there a discount for several cards?

    Yes: −5% from 3 cards · −10% from 6 cards, and a single shipment for the whole order. My selection works out the discount and shipping.

    What are lightning deals?

    A flash sale renewed every week: a few cards at a reduced price, including a graded one, for a few days. The struck-through price is the lowest of the previous 30 days; the end time is shown to the minute. These prices cannot be combined with quantity discounts. See the lightning deals.

    What does “1st Edition” mean?

    The card comes from the first print run of its set and bears the “1st Edition” logo (“1ED” in Japan). These copies are rarer and sought after: it is shown on the tile, the card page and the eBay listing.

    Can I collect a card in person?

    Yes, by appointment in Mandelieu-la-Napoule: you pay on eBay choosing local collection, and you see the card before taking it.

    Condition and authenticity

    What does “Near Mint” mean?

    Close to new: sharp corners and edges, no flaw visible to the naked eye. It is a guide: we send detailed photos of the actual card before any reservation.

    Where do the cards come from?

    Japanese cards are bought on the spot, in Japan, in specialist shops. For PSA-graded cards, the certificate number appears on the page when known, with a verification link to PSA’s website.

    Are the photos of the actual cards?

    Yes: we only publish our own photos, never official artwork. Until a listing has its photo, it shows a placeholder visual and we send photos on request.

    Returns and guarantees

    Do you accept returns?

    No: we do not take back or exchange cards, apart from the right of withdrawal that the law requires for distance purchases.

    How does the right of withdrawal work?

    For a distance purchase (paid on eBay), you have 14 days from receipt to withdraw: ask on eBay or use the form below. The card must come back exactly as it was sent: in its sleeve and toploader, reference sticker intact; slab intact, with the same certificate number; sealed product unopened. Return costs are yours. We refund after receiving and checking the card; any loss of value caused by handling (card taken out of its protection or damaged, seal opened) is deducted from the refund, up to its full amount.

    What about a purchase at a fair?

    French law gives no right of withdrawal for a purchase made at a fair or show; a sign on our stand says so. The same will apply to purchases in our shop.

    Which guarantees apply?

    A card that does not match its page or the eBay listing is covered: the legal guarantee of conformity, for 2 years from delivery (for a used card, a defect appearing within the first 12 months is presumed to have existed at the sale), the guarantee against hidden defects of the French Civil Code, and the eBay Money Back Guarantee.
